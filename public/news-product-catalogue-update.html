Product Catalogue Update: Tiger Grass, Nipa Palm and Bamboo Twig Brooms

PT Dharma Artha Nusantara has updated the product catalogue on naturalgrassbrooms.com to make it easier for international buyers to find and compare our natural brooms from Indonesia.

What’s in the updated catalogue

  • Tiger Grass Broom (Glagah / Rayung) — our main product line, now listed with the names used in international trade: tiger grass broom, soft grass broom and reed grass broom.
  • Nipa Palm Brooms (Nypah) — three models of 80, 105 and 120 cm, each with approximate weight and carton packing.
  • Bamboo Twig Broom — a natural outdoor broom for gardens and yards, also known as a bamboo garden broom.
  • Sorghum Broom — still under development; no specifications are published yet.

New: buyer FAQ and HS code information

The product page now includes a short FAQ for importers and distributors, covering the product names, the indicative HS code (9603.10) and how to request a quotation.

Request a quotation

Product details, packaging and commercial terms are confirmed for each enquiry. Share your product, quantity, packaging needs and destination through our quote form or on WhatsApp, and our team will reply with availability and terms.

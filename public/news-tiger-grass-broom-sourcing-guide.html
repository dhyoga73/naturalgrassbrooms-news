Tiger Grass Broom Sourcing Guide: How to Buy Tiger Grass Brooms from Indonesia

The tiger grass broom is one of the most recognisable natural brooms from Indonesia: light, soft and made by hand from dried grass. If you are an importer, wholesaler or retail buyer planning to source tiger grass brooms, this guide walks through the names you will meet, what to check before you order and how to send a request for quotation that gets a fast, accurate answer.

What is a tiger grass broom?

A tiger grass broom is a handmade broom whose head is made from bundles of dried grass, tied tightly and fanned out into soft, fine bristles. In Indonesia the same product is called sapu glagah or sapu rayung, so you may see it listed as a glagah broom or rayung broom. In export catalogues it is also sold as a soft grass broom or reed grass broom.

Different names, one product family. When you compare offers from several suppliers, make sure everyone is quoting the same type of broom: ask for photos of the broom head, the handle and the binding, not just a product name.

Why buyers choose tiger grass brooms

The soft, fine bristles of a tiger grass broom are well suited to sweeping smooth indoor floors such as tiles, wood and stone, and to collecting fine dust. Because the broom is made from natural plant material, it also fits retailers who want to offer plastic-free or natural cleaning products alongside their regular range.

For distributors, the tiger grass broom is a familiar everyday item in many Asian markets, which makes it a practical addition to a household products assortment.

What to check before you order

Natural products vary more than factory-made plastic ones, so agree on what "good" looks like before the first order. Useful points to discuss with your supplier:

  • Overall length and head width — confirm the measurements you need for your market and how much variation is acceptable.
  • Bristle density and softness — a fuller head sweeps better; ask the supplier to describe or photograph the density you will receive.
  • Binding — how the grass is tied to the handle and how securely it holds.
  • Handle — material, length and finish, and whether a hanging loop or label is required.
  • Dryness — natural fibres should be properly dried before packing so they arrive in good condition after a long sea journey.

The simplest way to align expectations is a sample. Check it against the points above and keep it as a reference for later shipments.

Packing questions to ask

Packing affects both product condition and landed cost. Ask how many tiger grass brooms go into one carton or bundle, the carton dimensions and gross weight, and whether the packing can be adapted to your requirements, for example individual labels or barcodes for retail. With carton details you can estimate freight and compare quotations on an equal basis.

For customs planning, brooms made of vegetable materials bound together are generally classified under HS 9603.10. Final classification is confirmed with the shipping documents and your country’s customs rules for each order.

How to request a quotation for tiger grass brooms

A clear RFQ saves several rounds of messages. Include:

  • Product: tiger grass broom (glagah / rayung), plus target length and handle type
  • Estimated quantity per order and expected order frequency
  • Packing and labelling requirements
  • Destination country and port
  • Preferred shipping terms (for example FOB or CIF) and target timing
  • Whether you need a sample first

FAQ

Is a glagah broom the same as a tiger grass broom?

Yes. Glagah and rayung are Indonesian names; tiger grass broom and soft grass broom are the names commonly used in export markets.

Can I order a sample first?

Ask us when you send your RFQ. A sample is the best way to agree on size, density and finish before a larger order.

Can I combine tiger grass brooms with other natural brooms?

Yes, you can ask about Nipa Palm brooms and Bamboo Twig brooms in the same enquiry.

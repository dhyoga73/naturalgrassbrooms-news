Indonesia is a long-standing source of handmade natural brooms. For importers, wholesalers and distributors, the challenge is often simple: the same broom can be known by several different names. This guide explains the main broom types we work with, the names used in international trade, and what to include when you request a quotation.

1. Tiger Grass Broom (Glagah / Rayung)

The glagah or rayung broom — sapu glagah or sapu rayung in Indonesian — is a traditional handmade broom made from dried grass. In international markets it is commonly sold as a tiger grass broom, soft grass broom or reed grass broom. Its soft, fine bristles make it a popular choice for sweeping indoor floors and collecting fine dust.

Glagah / Rayung is the main product line of PT Dharma Artha Nusantara. Length, weight, handle and packaging are confirmed for each enquiry based on the buyer’s market.

2. Nipa Palm Broom (Nypah)

Nipa palm brooms — known locally as sapu nypah — are natural brooms made from nipa palm material. We currently present three lengths, each with its own carton packing:

  • 80 cm — approx. 250 g, 100 pcs per carton
  • 105 cm — approx. 300 g, 50 pcs per carton
  • 120 cm — approx. 500 g, 40 pcs per carton

Weights are approximate. See the Nipa Palm models on our product page.

3. Bamboo Twig Broom

The bamboo twig broom — also called a bamboo garden broom or natural bamboo branch broom — is made from bundled bamboo twigs and branches. It is designed for outdoor use: gardens, yards, leaves and general outdoor sweeping. See the Bamboo Twig Broom details.

4. Sorghum Broom

Sorghum brooms are a product line we are still developing. Specifications and availability have not been published yet.

Which HS code applies?

Brooms made of twigs or other vegetable materials bound together are generally classified under HS 9603.10. Final classification is confirmed with the shipping documents and the destination country’s customs rules for each order.

What to include in your RFQ

To receive an accurate quotation faster, share as much of the following as you know:

  • Product type and size (for example, Tiger Grass broom or Nipa Palm 105 cm)
  • Estimated quantity per order and expected order frequency
  • Packaging and labelling requirements
  • Destination country and port
  • Preferred shipping terms and target timing
